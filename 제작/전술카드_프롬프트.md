# 전술 카드 프롬프트

ChatGPT·Gemini에 붙여 넣는 [전술 카드](../카드/전술카드.md) 그림 프롬프트. 계열마다 1개, 한 번에 6장씩 뽑는다.

- 그림에는 **카드 이름만** 넣고 효과 칸은 비워 둔다. 효과 글씨는 `카드_만들기.py`로 정확하게 넣는다. 그래서 밸런스 숫자가 바뀌어도 그림을 다시 뽑을 필요가 없다.
- 뽑은 그림은 `카드/` 폴더에 넣는다.
- 한글 이름이 틀리게 나오면 그 카드만 다시 뽑는다.

## 통합 프롬프트 (24장 한 번에)

이것 하나만 붙여 넣으면 된다. 계열마다 그림 1장씩, 모두 4장을 뽑는다. 한 번에 1장만 나오면 "next"라고 쳐서 다음 계열을 받는다. 계열별로 따로 뽑고 싶으면 아래 1~4번을 쓴다.

```
Create 4 separate images, one for each card family below. Each image is a sheet of 6 fantasy trading cards for a board game, arranged in a 3 × 2 grid (3 columns, 2 rows) on a plain solid white background, with a small even gap between cards. If you can only make one image per reply, make Family 1 now and make the next family each time I reply "next".

CARD STYLE (the same for all 24 cards)
- Portrait card, 63:88 proportions, ornate gilded gold frame, glowing sparkles, highly detailed digital painting, epic medieval fantasy. Same frame shape and layout on every card; only the family accent color, emblem, illustration and name change.
- Top left: a round gold medallion with the family emblem (no numbers).
- Top center: a small dark banner with the Korean text "전술 카드".
- Middle: a large illustration window (described per card below).
- Bottom: a dark banner with the family emblem as a small icon and the card name in large white Korean letters, exactly as written below.
- Under the name banner: an EMPTY dark rectangular text plate. Leave it completely blank. No text, letters or numbers inside it.

FAMILY 1 — 공격 (attack). Accent: crimson red enamel. Emblem: crossed swords.
1. "정면 공격": a wall of armored knights charging straight ahead across an open field, banners raised.
2. "돌격": a reckless cavalry charge crashing into enemy spears, dust and sparks flying.
3. "기습": soldiers bursting out of a forest at dawn onto a surprised camp.
4. "일제 사격": a line of archers releasing a dense volley of arrows that darkens the sky.
5. "화공": flaming arrows setting a jungle and wheat fields ablaze, orange firelight.
6. "광전사": a roaring berserker warrior with two axes, wild hair, red war paint, rage aura.

FAMILY 2 — 방어 (defense). Accent: steel silver and sapphire blue. Emblem: kite shield.
1. "방진": soldiers in a tight square formation with long spears pointing outward.
2. "농성": defenders on castle walls holding against a siege, ladders being pushed back.
3. "후퇴": an orderly army retreating across a bridge at dusk while a rear guard holds.
4. "방패벽": a row of interlocked tower shields with arrows bouncing off them.
5. "참호": soldiers crouching in deep earthen trenches behind wooden stakes.
6. "산악 수비": defenders on a rocky mountain pass rolling boulders down on attackers.

FAMILY 3 — 책략 (strategy). Accent: deep royal purple. Emblem: all-seeing eye.
1. "계략": a cloaked strategist moving pieces on a war map by candlelight.
2. "매복": soldiers hidden among jungle leaves and mountain rocks, eyes glinting.
3. "첩보": a hooded spy on a rooftop at night reading a stolen letter.
4. "위장 퇴각": an army pretending to flee while hidden troops wait behind a hill.
5. "야습": shadowy soldiers sneaking into a sleeping camp under a full moon.
6. "보급 차단": saboteurs burning enemy supply wagons on a narrow road.

FAMILY 4 — 기동 (maneuver). Accent: emerald green. Emblem: winged horse.
1. "측면 공격": cavalry sweeping around the side of an enemy line, seen from above.
2. "강행군": a long column of soldiers marching fast through rain and mud.
3. "도하": soldiers crossing a wide river on rafts and a pontoon bridge.
4. "기병 돌파": heavy cavalry smashing through the center of an enemy formation.
5. "해상 상륙": warships landing troops on a beach, boats hitting the sand.
6. "우회": a small army taking a secret mountain path around an enemy fortress.

RULES
- Cards in each image go left to right, top row first, in the order listed.
- Every card name must be spelled exactly as given, in Korean.
- No other text anywhere except "전술 카드" and the card name.
- Keep the frame design identical across all 4 images so the 24 cards look like one set.
```
