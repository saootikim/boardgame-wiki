# 기후 카드 프롬프트

ChatGPT·Gemini에 붙여 넣는 [기후 카드](../카드/기후카드.md) 12장 그림 프롬프트. 한 번에 6장씩, 그림 2장을 뽑는다.

- 크기: 다른 카드와 같은 세로 카드(63:88 비율)로 뽑고, 인쇄할 때는 모든 카드처럼 44×63mm로 줄인다. 재앙 카드도 같은 크기로 만든다.
- 그림 속 장면은 카드 효과를 보여 준다 (예: 눈 = 눈길을 힘들게 걷는 군대, 이동 주사위 -1).
- 그림에는 **카드 이름만** 넣고 효과 칸은 비워 둔다. 효과 글씨는 `카드_만들기.py`로 정확하게 넣는다. 그래서 효과 숫자가 바뀌어도 그림을 다시 뽑을 필요가 없다.
- 뽑은 그림은 `카드/` 폴더에 넣는다. 한글 이름이 틀리게 나오면 그 장만 다시 뽑는다.

## 통합 프롬프트 (12장)

```
Create 2 separate images. Each image is a sheet of 6 fantasy trading cards for a board game, arranged in a 3 × 2 grid (3 columns, 2 rows) on a plain solid white background, with a small even gap between cards. If you can only make one image per reply, make Sheet 1 now and make Sheet 2 when I reply "next".

CARD STYLE (the same for all 12 cards)
- Portrait card, 63:88 proportions, ornate gilded gold frame with sky blue and pearl white enamel accents, glowing sparkles, highly detailed digital painting, epic medieval fantasy. Same frame shape and layout on every card; only the illustration and name change.
- Top left: a round gold medallion with a sun-behind-a-cloud emblem (no numbers).
- Top center: a small dark banner with the Korean text "기후 카드".
- Middle: a large illustration window showing the weather scene described below. The weather must fill the whole sky and landscape so the card is readable at a glance.
- Bottom: a dark banner with the small sun-and-cloud icon and the card name in large white Korean letters, exactly as written below.
- Under the name banner: an EMPTY dark rectangular text plate. Leave it completely blank. No text, letters or numbers inside it.

SHEET 1
1. "화창함": a bright golden sun in a clear blue sky over a happy village, farmers carrying full sacks of grain.
2. "비": steady rain over a swelling river and lush green wheat fields, explorers in a boat following the river.
3. "눈": heavy snowfall, an army trudging slowly knee-deep in snow on a white road, horses struggling.
4. "구름 낀": a calm grey overcast sky over quiet farmland, an ordinary peaceful day, nothing happening.
5. "번개 치는": a violent thunderstorm over a battlefield, a bolt of lightning striking near attackers while defenders hold a hill.
6. "지중해성": a warm sunny coastal market town with white houses and olive trees, a merchant raising prices at his stall.

SHEET 2
1. "안개": thick white fog over a battlefield, shadowy soldiers half-hidden, a commander secretly swapping a face-down card.
2. "강풍": a strong wind filling the sails of warships racing over choppy waves, flags snapping.
3. "가뭄 기미": early signs of drought, cracked dry earth at the edge of fields, a pale hazy sun, a tax collector closing an empty ledger.
4. "풍년": an overflowing harvest, golden wheat fields and jungle trees heavy with fruit, baskets piled high.
5. "오로라": a glowing green and violet aurora over a frozen wasteland, explorers meeting a native tribe beside their tents.
6. "붉은 하늘": an ominous blood-red sky over the horizon, a dark shape of a coming disaster in the clouds, people pointing in fear.

RULES
- Cards in each image go left to right, top row first, in the order listed.
- Every card name must be spelled exactly as given, in Korean.
- No other text anywhere except "기후 카드" and the card name.
- Keep the frame design identical across both images so the 12 cards look like one set.
```
