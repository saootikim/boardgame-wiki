# 이념 카드 프롬프트

ChatGPT·Gemini에 붙여 넣는 [이념 카드](../세계/이념카드.md) 5장 그림 프롬프트. 그림 1장에 3×2로 뽑고, 오른쪽 아래 칸은 비워 둔다.

- 크기와 틀은 [기후 카드 프롬프트](기후카드_프롬프트.md)와 같다 (63:88 세로 카드, 인쇄 44×63mm). 색은 검정과 금색이다.
- 실제 나라 깃발, 정치 상징(하켄크로이츠 등), 실존 인물(무솔리니 등)은 넣지 않는다. 이미지 AI가 거절하거나 그림을 망친다. 그래서 장면은 판타지 비유로 그렸다.
- 효과 칸은 비워 둔다. 문구와 효과 글씨는 `카드_만들기.py`로 넣는다.
- 뽑은 그림은 `카드/이념카드.png`로 넣는다.

## 통합 프롬프트 (5장)

```
Create one image: a sheet of fantasy trading cards for a board game, arranged in a 3 × 2 grid (3 columns, 2 rows) on a plain solid white background, with a small even gap between cards. There are only 5 cards: leave the bottom-right slot completely empty and white.

CARD STYLE (the same for all 5 cards)
- Portrait card, 63:88 proportions, ornate gilded gold frame with black lacquer and ivory enamel accents, regal and propaganda-poster mood, highly detailed digital painting, epic medieval fantasy. Same frame shape and layout on every card; only the illustration and name change.
- Top left: a round gold medallion with a laurel crown emblem (no numbers).
- Top center: a small dark banner with the Korean text "이념 카드".
- Middle: a large illustration window showing the scene described below.
- Bottom: a dark banner with the small laurel icon and the card name in large white Korean letters, exactly as written below.
- Under the name banner: an EMPTY dark rectangular text plate. Leave it completely blank. No text, letters or numbers inside it.

CARDS
1. "독일": a gleaming workshop of brass gears and great machines, engineers at work, a masked lord in the shadows offering a glowing contract bound with golden chains. A faint violet glow marks it as a special, rare card.
2. "나치": a grim iron fortress where sacks of grain are poured into a great machine and rows of grey-armored soldiers march out of it. Iron banners with a plain black gear emblem only.
3. "파시즘": a fictional stout general in a black cloak happily hugging a giant pile of golden grain sheaves at a harvest festival, comic and warm.
4. "사회주의": villagers splitting every pile of grain exactly in half on a long table, red banners with a plain wheat emblem.
5. "자본주의": top-hatted merchants counting gold coins in a foggy harbor city, thin puppet strings rising from their hands into the dark sky.

RULES
- Cards go left to right, top row first, in the order listed.
- Every card name must be spelled exactly as given, in Korean.
- No other text anywhere except "이념 카드" and the card name.
- No real-world flags, political symbols or real people. Use only the invented fantasy emblems described above.
```
