# 실사 지도 프롬프트

ChatGPT 이미지 생성과 Gemini에 그대로 붙여 넣는 통합 프롬프트. 미리보기 그림은 첨부하지 않아도 된다.
배치는 [보드판](보드판.md) 초안과 같고, 칸은 7.5mm(72×48)다.

- 칸 크기를 바꾸려면 `72 columns × 48 rows`(두 군데)를 고친다: 10mm는 `54 × 36`, 5mm는 `108 × 72`.
- AI가 칸 개수를 틀리거나 인쇄할 때 흐리면, `GRID` 부분을 지우고 칸 없는 그림을 받아 정확한 칸을 따로 씌운다.

```
Create a photorealistic top-down satellite image of an original fantasy world. It will be printed as a board game map across 4 A4 sheets, so every region must be clearly readable from directly above.

FORMAT
- Landscape, exact 3:2 aspect ratio. Highest resolution available.
- Pure top-down (nadir) view. No perspective, no tilt, no horizon.
- The map fills the entire frame edge to edge. No frame, border, margin, title, legend, compass, scale bar, labels, letters, numbers or symbols anywhere.

GRID
- Overlay a perfectly even square grid on the whole image: exactly 72 columns × 48 rows.
- Every cell is the same size and perfectly square. Lines are straight, thin, dark grey at about 35% opacity, and run edge to edge with no gaps.
- The grid sits on top of the terrain. The terrain stays fully visible through it.

VISUAL STYLE
- Real satellite photography look: natural colors, soft sunlight from the upper left, gentle shadows that show terrain relief.
- Coastlines are irregular and natural: beaches, rocky cliffs, a bright turquoise shallow shelf along every coast that fades into deep blue open ocean.
- Clouds appear only over the Bermuda Triangle. Everywhere else is clear.
- Every region below has a crisp, visible edge so players can tell which grid cell belongs to which terrain.

LAYOUT (positions as % of image width from the left, x, and % of height from the top, y)

Polar regions
- North pole: a white ice cap covering the top 9% of the image, with crevasses, snowdrifts and an irregular icy edge. One small ice tongue reaches down near x 14%.
- South pole: the same ice cap covering the bottom 9%, with small ice tongues near x 20% and x 77%.

Open ocean
- Everything not described below is open ocean: natural blue water with faint wave texture.

West continent: spans x 3–34%, y 15–78%. Wider in the north, narrowing to the south.
- Mountain range: x 10–19%, y 20–35%. Rocky grey-brown peaks with snow on the summits.
- Open-pit mine in the middle of that range at x 16%, y 24%: terraced grey quarry pit with dirt roads.
- River: starts in the mountains at x 24%, y 24%, meanders southwest and reaches the west coast at x 2%, y 54%, ending in a small delta.
- Golden wheat farmland beside the river: x 7–11%, y 35–41%. Neat rectangular field patches in yellow and gold tones.
- Second wheat farmland: x 21–26%, y 65–72%.
- Dense tropical jungle: x 19–31%, y 41–54%. Thick dark green canopy.
- Merchant town near the east coast at x 30%, y 33%: a small medieval town of red and brown rooftops, a market square, roads leading out, and a tiny harbor.
- Southern mountains: x 9–16%, y 59–70%, with a second open-pit mine at x 13%, y 63%.
- All remaining land is green grassland and plains with subtle field and forest texture.

East continent: spans x 64–93%, y 15–78%.
- Merchant town in the northwest at x 70%, y 28%: same style as the west town.
- Mountain range: x 79–89%, y 20–30%, snow-capped, with an open-pit mine at x 81%, y 24%.
- Volcanic wasteland: x 84–90%, y 37–46%. Black cracked basalt, glowing orange lava rivers and pools, thin smoke, and a sharp edge where it meets the grassland.
- River: starts at x 79%, y 33%, flows south through the middle of the continent and reaches the south coast at x 76%, y 78%.
- Golden wheat farmland: x 69–73%, y 37–41%, and a second patch at x 78–83%, y 65–72%.
- Dense tropical jungle: x 76–85%, y 50–59%.
- Southeast mountains: x 86–92%, y 59–68%, with an open-pit mine at x 87%, y 63%.
- All remaining land is green grassland and plains.

Central ocean, between the continents
- Deep ocean trench: a large region at x 40–60%, y 22–52%. Very dark navy, abyssal water, clearly darker than the surrounding ocean.
- Sunken sea temple at the exact center of the trench, x 50%, y 37%: ancient white stone ruins with columns and a circular plaza, faintly visible under glowing teal water.
- Bermuda Triangle: a region at x 40–60%, y 59–83%. Stormy grey-violet water, swirling currents, a whirlpool, patches of mist and low storm clouds.
- Three small sandy islands inside it, forming a triangle: x 44%, y 63%; x 56%, y 63%; x 50%, y 76%.

Small islands (sandy beaches with a little green vegetation)
- x 50%, y 15%
- x 33%, y 63%
- x 10%, y 85%
- x 90%, y 80%

CHECK BEFORE FINISHING
- No text or symbols anywhere in the image.
- Both continents, the trench, the Bermuda Triangle and both ice caps are in the positions above.
- The grid covers the whole image evenly: 72 columns × 48 rows.
```
