# 재앙 카드 프롬프트

ChatGPT·Gemini에 붙여 넣는 [재앙 카드](../카드/재앙카드.md) 12장 그림 프롬프트. 한 번에 6장씩, 그림 2장을 뽑는다.

- 크기와 틀은 [기후 카드 프롬프트](기후카드_프롬프트.md)와 같다 (63:88 세로 카드, 인쇄 44×63mm). 색만 다르다: 재앙은 진홍과 흑요석색이다.
- 그림 속 장면은 카드 효과를 보여 준다. 효과 칸은 비워 두고, 글씨는 `카드_만들기.py`로 넣는다.
- 레드문의 7대죄 주사위는 카드가 아니라 진짜 주사위로 굴린다. 그래서 레드문 카드 그림에만 주사위를 넣는다.
- 뽑은 그림은 `카드/재앙카드_1.png`, `카드/재앙카드_2.png`로 넣는다.

## 통합 프롬프트 (12장)

```
Create 2 separate images. Each image is a sheet of 6 fantasy trading cards for a board game, arranged in a 3 × 2 grid (3 columns, 2 rows) on a plain solid white background, with a small even gap between cards. If you can only make one image per reply, make Sheet 1 now and make Sheet 2 when I reply "next".

CARD STYLE (the same for all 12 cards)
- Portrait card, 63:88 proportions, ornate gilded gold frame with dark crimson and black obsidian enamel accents, cracks of glowing red light in the frame, highly detailed digital painting, epic dark medieval fantasy. Same frame shape and layout on every card; only the illustration and name change.
- Top left: a round gold medallion with a falling flaming comet emblem (no numbers).
- Top center: a small dark banner with the Korean text "재앙 카드".
- Middle: a large illustration window showing the catastrophe described below, dramatic and dangerous, filling the whole scene.
- Bottom: a dark banner with the small comet icon and the card name in large white Korean letters, exactly as written below.
- Under the name banner: an EMPTY dark rectangular text plate. Leave it completely blank. No text, letters or numbers inside it.

SHEET 1
1. "빙하기": endless glaciers swallowing farmland, frozen wheat fields, an army walking straight across a frozen river.
2. "홍수": the sea surging over the coast, giant waves swallowing seaside villages and fields.
3. "산사태": a mountainside collapsing, boulders and mud burying the villages at its feet.
4. "전염병": a plague-stricken army camp, sick soldiers lying in tents, plague doctors with bird masks.
5. "화산폭발": an erupting volcano raining fire and ash, an army fleeing from the burning mountain.
6. "폭염": a scorching white sun and heat haze over cracked land, exhausted settlers struggling to plant a flag.

SHEET 2
1. "한파": a freezing blizzard, explorers turning back on a frozen road, frost covering their cloaks.
2. "좀비사태": hordes of zombies rising from the earth and shambling toward a walled village at dusk.
3. "가뭄": the sea pulled far back from the shore, ships stranded on the cracked dry seabed, withered crops.
4. "소행성": a huge flaming asteroid striking the land, a glowing crater and a shockwave of fire.
5. "레드문": a giant blood-red moon over the world, and a glowing seven-sided die floating in the red sky.
6. "지진": the ground splitting open between two armies, soldiers lowering their swords as the earth shakes.

RULES
- Cards in each image go left to right, top row first, in the order listed.
- Every card name must be spelled exactly as given, in Korean.
- No other text anywhere except "재앙 카드" and the card name.
- Keep the frame design identical across both images so the 12 cards look like one set.
```
