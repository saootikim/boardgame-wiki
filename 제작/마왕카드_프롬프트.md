# 마왕 카드 프롬프트

ChatGPT·Gemini에 붙여 넣는 [마왕 카드](../카드/마왕카드.md) 6장 그림 프롬프트. 그림 1장에 3×2로 뽑는다.

- 크기와 틀은 [재앙 카드 프롬프트](재앙카드_프롬프트.md)와 같다 (63:88 세로 카드, 인쇄 44×63mm). 색은 검보라와 녹색 귀화(유령 불)다.
- 그림 속 장면은 카드 효과를 보여 준다. 효과 칸은 비워 두고, 글씨는 `카드_만들기.py`로 넣는다.
- 뽑은 그림은 `카드/마왕카드.png`로 넣는다. 순서: 윗줄 흉작의 저주·시체 행군·공포, 아랫줄 역병·배신의 속삭임·무덤 깨우기.
- 뒷면은 아래 "뒷면 프롬프트"로 따로 1장 뽑는다. 다른 덱 뒷면([카드 뒷면 프롬프트](카드뒷면_프롬프트.md))과 같은 틀이다.

## 통합 프롬프트 (6장)

```
Create one image: a sheet of 6 fantasy trading cards for a board game, arranged in a 3 × 2 grid (3 columns, 2 rows) on a plain solid white background, with a small even gap between cards.

CARD STYLE (the same for all 6 cards)
- Portrait card, 63:88 proportions, ornate dark iron and tarnished gold frame with deep violet and black enamel accents, thorny spikes and glowing green ghost-fire in the frame, highly detailed digital painting, epic dark medieval fantasy, sinister and cursed mood. Same frame shape and layout on every card; only the illustration and name change.
- Top left: a round dark gold medallion with a horned demon skull emblem (no numbers).
- Top center: a small dark banner with the Korean text "마왕 카드".
- Middle: a large illustration window showing the curse described below, dark and menacing, filling the whole scene.
- Bottom: a dark banner with the small horned skull icon and the card name in large white Korean letters, exactly as written below.
- Under the name banner: an EMPTY dark rectangular text plate. Leave it completely blank. No text, letters or numbers inside it.

CARDS
1. "흉작의 저주": a shadowy demon hand reaching over golden wheat fields, the wheat withering and turning black where its shadow falls, farmers in despair.
2. "시체 행군": a horde of zombies marching fast down a road at night under a green moon, a demon general on a skeletal horse driving them on.
3. "공포": a king on his throne frozen in terror, a giant demon silhouette looming over the castle, a war banner dropped on the floor.
4. "역병": green toxic mist rolling over an army camp, soldiers collapsing and rising again as pale zombies.
5. "배신의 속삭임": a hooded demon whispering into the ear of one of two kings shaking hands, a dagger hidden behind his back, a torn treaty scroll.
6. "무덤 깨우기": a dark graveyard in a hellish land, the ground cracking open, zombie hands bursting out of the graves under green ghost-fire.

RULES
- Cards go left to right, top row first, in the order listed.
- Every card name must be spelled exactly as given, in Korean.
- No other text anywhere except "마왕 카드" and the card name.
- No real flags, political symbols or real people.
```

## 뒷면 프롬프트 (1장)

```
Create one image: a single fantasy card BACK for a board game, on a plain solid white background.

- Portrait card, 63:88 proportions, ornate gilded gold frame, highly detailed digital painting, epic dark medieval fantasy, rich and luxurious like a premium trading card back.
- The whole design is perfectly symmetrical, left-right AND top-bottom, so it looks the same upside down.
- Background: a deep black-violet field with a fine engraved gold filigree pattern (arabesque scrollwork) covering the whole card, faint glowing green ghost-fire in the pattern.
- Center: a large round gold medallion with a horned demon skull emblem, glowing softly green.
- Directly under the medallion: an EMPTY dark ribbon banner. Leave it completely blank.
- Thin double gold border around the edge, ornate gold corner pieces in all 4 corners.
- No text, letters or numbers anywhere.
```
